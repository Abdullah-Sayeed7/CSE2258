About CodeQuiz

CodeQuiz is a quiz website for students. Each topic has an Easy quiz with simple questions and a Hard quiz with tricky questions. Submit your quiz to see your result.

How It Works

1. Choose a Topic

Open the Topics page and pick a subject.

2. Choose a Level

Select Easy (10 questions) or Hard (15 questions).

3. Submit the Quiz

Answer all questions and press Submit to see your result.

Instructions

  • Each question has four options. Choose only one.
  • Easy quiz: 10 questions, 20 minutes.
  • Hard quiz: 15 questions, 30 minutes.
  • Once a quiz has started, you cannot go to another page until you submit it.
  • Do not refresh the page during a quiz.
